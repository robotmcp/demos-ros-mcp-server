FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
================================================================================

Q: Where are the mesh files?
A: You must provide them from your original Go2 model.
   Place them in: go2_description/meshes/

Q: What's the default world?
A: empty.sdf - good for starting out

Q: How do I launch a different world?
A: ros2 launch go2_description gazebo.launch.py world:=obstacles

Q: How do I visualize the robot?
A: rviz2 -d $(ros2 pkg prefix go2_description)/config/go2.rviz

Q: How do I control the joints?
A: Publish to /go2/JOINT_NAME_controller/command topics

Q: What if simulation runs slow?
A: Use empty.sdf world or run headless (export HEADLESS=1)

Q: Is this for ROS2 or ROS1?
A: ROS2 only (Humble/Iron)

Q: Which Gazebo version should I use?
A: Gazebo 6+ (Fortress or newer)
